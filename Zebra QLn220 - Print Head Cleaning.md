# Zebra QLn220 — Print Head Cleaning Instructions

Source: Zebra QLn Series User Guide (official), "General Cleaning Instructions" and "QLn Series Cleaning" sections.

## Before you start

- Always power off the printer before cleaning.
- The print head can be very hot after printing — let it cool first.
- Only use a Zebra cleaning pen or a cotton swab dampened with 70% isopropyl alcohol. Zebra explicitly warns against any other cleaning agent — it can damage the print elements and isn't covered under warranty.
- Never insert anything sharp or pointed into the printer.

## Steps

1. Power off the printer and let the print head cool if it's been printing recently.
2. Press the latch release lever on the side of the printer and open the media door to expose the print head.
3. Wipe the print elements end to end with the cleaning pen or an isopropyl-alcohol-dampened swab. The print elements are the thin gray line running across the print head.
4. Rotate the platen roller (the black rubber roller the label feeds over) and clean it the same way — a dirty platen can cause the same light/patchy print symptoms as a dirty head.
5. Let everything air dry for a minute before closing the door and printing again.

## Cleaning frequency (Zebra's own guidance)

- Print head and platen roller: after every 5 rolls of media, or sooner if you're seeing print quality problems.
- Linerless media: after every roll.

## Note on this printer specifically

The QLn220 is direct thermal (no ribbon), so ribbon wrinkling/alignment isn't a factor here — it's purely head cleanliness, print darkness/speed settings, and label stock contact against the head.

A clogged or dirty head usually shows up as streaks or blank vertical lines running the same way across the *whole* label, not one specific text element being uniformly lighter while everything else prints fine. If cleaning doesn't fix a specific element (like the product name) printing lighter than the rest, that's more likely the darkness/speed settings than the head itself.
